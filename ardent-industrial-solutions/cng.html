CNG is usually cheaper than diesel. Ardent Industrial Solutions Limited sells CNG generators and cascades, and converts existing diesel generators to run on diesel and gas.

CNG Generators

A CNG generator produces electricity from compressed natural gas instead of diesel or petrol. It suits businesses and facilities that want gas as their source of power.

Tell us your load and running hours, and we will match a generator to your site.

CNG Cascades

A cascade is a bank of high-pressure cylinders connected together in a steel frame. Cascades are filled at a compression station, delivered by road, and connected to your generator.

This brings gas to sites without a pipeline connection. The number of cascades you need depends on how much gas your site uses.

Diesel Dual Fuel (DDF)

DDF means a diesel engine is modified to run on two fuels: CNG as the main fuel, and diesel for ignition. CNG cannot self-ignite in a diesel engine, so diesel is reduced, not removed.

The DDF ratio

The ratio shows how much of the engine's energy comes from CNG and how much from diesel. An engine that used 10 litres of diesel per hour may use only 4 litres after conversion, with CNG replacing the rest.

60% CNG 40% Diesel

What to expect

  • Most systems reach 50% to 80% CNG substitution.
  • High load means more CNG. Low load needs more diesel.
  • The engine cannot run 100% on CNG in DDF mode.
  • The more CNG you use, the more you can save.

Think of diesel as the lighter and CNG as the cooking gas. You still need the lighter to start the flame, but the gas does most of the cooking.
