CNG Generators & Dual Fuel

  • C.

    Sell CNG Generators And CNG Cascades For Businesses And Facilities Seeking Gas Power. Cascades Are Banks Of High-pressure Cylinders, Filled At Compression Stations And Delivered By Road, Supplying Gas To Generators At Sites Without A Pipeline Connection.

  • D.

    Convert Existing Diesel Generators To Dual Fuel Generator Sets. The Generator Starts On Diesel, Then Runs On A Mix Of Natural Gas And A Small Amount Of Diesel, Reducing Diesel Consumption Without Replacing The Generator.
