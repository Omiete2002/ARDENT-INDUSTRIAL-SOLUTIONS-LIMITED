About ARDENT INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS LIMITED

Welcome to ARDENT INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS LIMITED.

We are a dynamic, multi-sector indigenous company incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 (CAMA), dedicated to delivering world-class infrastructure, engineering, and procurement solutions across Nigeria and beyond.

With our corporate headquarters situated in the heart of Ikoyi, Lagos, and a strategic regional office in Abuja, we are positioned at the center of Nigeria's commercial and administrative hubs to effectively serve both private and public entities.

Our Core Capabilities

Ardent Industrial Solutions is built to be a one-stop powerhouse for heavy industry and national development. Our mandate spans a vast array of sectors, allowing us to provide end-to-end solutions for complex projects:

  • Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC)

    We offer comprehensive civil, electrical, mechanical, and structural engineering services. From the construction of smart cities, high-rises, highways, and bridges, to full-scale project management, we bring monumental visions to life.

  • Oil & Gas Services

    We provide technical, mechanical, and logistical support to the upstream, midstream, and downstream sectors. Our expertise includes seismic data acquisition, well drilling, pipeline laying, and the distribution of refined fuels and LNG.

  • General Contracting & Procurement

    We act as independent providers of general procurement services, supplying essential commodities, industrial equipment, and materials required by large-scale private and public entities.

  • Agriculture & Agro-Processing

    We are actively involved in large-scale agricultural operations, including crop cultivation, livestock, and agro-processing, supported by modern cold-storage and value-chain manufacturing systems.

  • Real Estate & Industrial Manufacturing

    We design, fabricate, and manufacture specialized industrial equipment, while also acquiring and developing prime real estate and civil works necessary to drive economic growth.

Our Vision

To be the premier industrial solutions provider in Africa, executing projects with absolute precision, safety, and reliability. We are committed to bridging the gap between heavy industry and sustainable national development.

Our Leadership

Our operations are guided by a highly capable board of directors, Mr. Bashir Jamil Mohammed and Mr. Grema Ashiekh Mohammed. Under their leadership, Ardent Industrial Solutions Limited is driven by a commitment to excellence, integrity, and the relentless pursuit of multi-sector innovation.

Why Partner With Us?

Whether you require a general contractor for a mega-infrastructure project, a reliable partner for oilfield services, or a procurement manager for industrial goods, ARDENT INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS LIMITED has the legal framework, the industry mandate, and the operational capacity to deliver.
